BGA Gastro Fusión is the largest gastronomic fair in the department of Santander, Colombia. This event has established itself as a key meeting point for the HORECA sector (Hotels, Restaurants, and Cafés), local entrepreneurs, and food lovers in the region.

Main Events and Activities

The festival offers a diverse program that in past editions and for the 2026 edition includes:

  • Live Cooking Shows: Demonstrations by local, national, and international chefs on main stages.
  • Bartender and Mixology Battles: Competitions among the best cocktail experts in the region.
  • Workshops and Talks: Academic and practical sessions to learn about trends, ingredients, and culinary techniques.
  • Spirits Tastings: Guided tasting sessions of wines, craft beers, and tequilas.
  • Experience Zone: Areas dedicated to specialty coffees, pastry, traditional sweets, and a supplier area with machinery and ingredients.

What is “BGA Gastro Fusión”?

BGA Gastro Fusión is a gastronomic festival in Bucaramanga (Colombia) focused on bringing together the best of food, drinks, and entertainment in one place.

📍 Basic event information

  • Name: BGA Gastro Fusión
  • City: Bucaramanga (BGA)
  • Venue: Neomundo (event center)
  • Dates (example edition): September 15, 16, and 17
  • Type: Gastronomic and cultural festival

What can you find at the festival?

🍔 Food

  • Local restaurants and food brands
  • Innovative dishes (fusion cuisine)
  • Tastings

🍷 Drinks

  • Wine, beer, and liquor tastings
  • Cocktail experiences

Purpose of the festival

  • Promote local gastronomy
  • Support entrepreneurs and restaurants
  • Attract tourism to Bucaramanga
  • Create social and cultural experiences
